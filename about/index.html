About the Artist


Sarah Belsky is a passionate amateur artist working across drawing, painting, and digital media. Professionally trained as a genetic counselor, she balances a career in genetics with a lifelong dedication to the arts. Originally from the United States, she now lives in Paris, where museums and contemporary art continue to shape her creative exploration.
